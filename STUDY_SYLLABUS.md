# Quantitative Aptitude Study Syllabus

This repository contains the study plan for quantitative aptitude practice.

## Topics

1. Number Systems
2. BODMAS
3. Decimals
4. Fractions
5. LCM and HCF
6. Ratio and Proportion
7. Percentages
8. Mensuration
9. Time and Work
10. Time and Distance
11. Simple and Compound Interest
12. Profit and Loss
13. Algebra
14. Geometry
15. Trigonometry
16. Elementary Statistics
17. Square Root
18. Age Calculations
19. Calendar and Clock
20. Pipes and Cisterns

## Study method

For each topic, study:

- Core concepts and formulas
- Worked examples
- Shortcuts and common patterns
- Practice questions
- A review of mistakes

Recommended starting point: **Number Systems**.
